Infographic — BRICS v G7 Demographics

![Infographic](BRICS v G7.png)
Think about it or not. Agree with it or not. Great-power competition is back, and we may well be entering a new Cold War. In this context, it is worth comparing the two major economic blocs that have taken shape in the first quarter of the 21st century: on one side, the BRICS; on the other, the G7.

This infographic highlights two key demographic dimensions of that rivalry. On the left, births per woman across BRICS and G7 countries show a broad decline since 2018. The main exception is the United Arab Emirates. One could argue that Germany also deviates slightly, but the change there is so small as to be almost negligible. Today, the only countries with fertility rates above two births per woman are all found within the BRICS group.

On the right, the population pie chart reveals the deeper structural shift behind these trends: BRICS countries now account for more than half of the world’s population, while the G7 represent just about 10 percent. In demographic terms, the balance of the world is no longer centered where it once was.
